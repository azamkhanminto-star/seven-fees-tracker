# SEVEN Career Institute: 10-second Runway reel

Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), 10 s, for Instagram Reels and WhatsApp Status.
Runway makes the **footage only**. The exact text, the SEVEN logo and the music are added
afterwards in code (Remotion), because AI video misspells words and warps logos.

## Story (10 s)

| Time | Shot (Runway) | On-screen text (added in code) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Shot 1: a student stuck on a maths problem | "Stuck in Maths?" |
| 3–6.5 s | Shot 2: the same kind of student understanding, smiling | "We find the **real gap** first." |
| 6.5–10 s | Shot 3: warm classroom, teacher and students | SEVEN logo · "FREE Root Diagnostic Test" · Medical Road, Aligarh |

## Runway prompts (Gen-4, 9:16, 5 s each; trim in the edit)

**Shot 1: the problem**
> Close-up of an Indian teenage student at a wooden study desk at night, frowning at a maths notebook, pencil tapping, warm desk lamp, shallow depth of field, slow push-in, cinematic, soft teal and warm amber tones, realistic, no text.

**Shot 2: the breakthrough**
> Same style: Indian teenage student's face lights up with understanding, small confident smile, looks up from notebook, warm morning light from window, slow orbit camera move, cinematic, teal and white colour palette, realistic, no text.

**Shot 3: the institute**
> Bright modern coaching classroom in India, a friendly teacher explaining at a whiteboard, students in school uniform engaged and smiling, gentle dolly-out, clean teal and white interior, natural light, cinematic, realistic, no text, no logos.

Tips: add "no text, no logos" to every prompt; generate two or three takes of each shot and pick the best; keep the camera moves slow so the text stays readable.

## Credits (Runway)
3 shots × 5 s. Runway's price depends on the model and your plan, so check its cost
estimate before generating. Allow for two or three takes per shot.

## After the footage exists
Drop the three clips into `video/public/runway/` (shot1.mp4, shot2.mp4, shot3.mp4) and the
code adds: brand-colour text, the animated SEVEN logo, an original 10 s music sting and the
loudness and WhatsApp exports.
